## Still requires custom work

- **Full 24 light executions:** Six distinct source cuts are adapted. The designed twelve directions with two individually authored executions each are not all present; do not relabel mirrored or repeated clips as new executions.
- **Six distinct heavy executions:** Three heavy source cuts are adapted. The second individually authored execution for each designed heavy direction is still required.
- **Five paired finishers and victim tracks:** The downloaded set has no matching paired chest-cleave/disarm/throat-cut/dismemberment finishers. They need custom paired choreography, contacts, prop/gore timing and camera paths.
- **Dedicated straight thrust / thrust riposte:** Phase A has a stationary authored extension study; this single-handed library currently supplies a dash-cut candidate rather than mislabelling a cut as a thrust.
- **Omnidirectional block/parry interception:** One block/parry family is adapted. Target-relative interception and distinct rear/side poses still need authoring and validation.
- **Pose-matched interruptions, hit outcomes and chains:** Individual actions preview correctly in isolation; arbitrary live transitions and shared contact/damage markers require combat integration.
- **Final locomotion/roll contacts, armor/finger/cloth and gore polish:** Review candidates are not final production acceptance. No damage, invulnerability, slow motion, enemy coordination or sword-drop behavior is added by this library.
- **Turns, slope/collision contacts and movement handoffs:** Eight-way movement/evasion and guarded movement are previewed. Target-facing turns, sprint curves, run-to-attack braking, phase-matched starts/stops, slopes and collision-shortened travel still require live-controller contact work.
